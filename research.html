Research

Work in mathematics, scientific computing, and machine learning.

Mathematics

Cofinite Zeros of High Derivatives. Independent research, 2026. Constructed a transcendental entire function whose sufficiently high derivatives have a zero in every prescribed nonempty open subset of the complex plane. The proof has a complete Lean 4 formalization. PDF · Lean formalization

Machine Learning

Local Regularization Does Not Characterize Multiclass PAC Learnability. Independent research, 2026. Gives a negative answer to whether local regularization characterizes multiclass PAC learnability, via a countable class of bounded Daniely-Shalev-Shwartz dimension that no local regularizer learns. PDF

Scientific Computing

Approximation-Free Diffusion Posterior Sampling. Stanford University, 2026-present. Working with a Stanford professor and Ph.D. student on approximation-free diffusion posterior sampling for scientific inverse problems. Applied the method to InverseBench tasks including linear inverse scattering, the Navier-Stokes equations, and full-waveform inversion.
